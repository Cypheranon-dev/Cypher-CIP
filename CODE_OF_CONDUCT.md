# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this to be a place where people can disagree sharply about the
cryptography and the engineering without it turning personal. Critique the
work, not the person. That's the whole policy, expanded a little below.

## Expected

- Direct, technical disagreement — including blunt disagreement — is welcome
- Admitting a mistake publicly is treated as a good thing, not a weakness
- Assume good faith on a first interaction; adjust based on evidence
- Keep identity out of it — this is a pseudonymous project by design, and
  that courtesy extends to contributors who choose to stay pseudonymous too

## Not acceptable

- Personal attacks, harassment, or targeting someone rather than their argument
- Publishing someone's private information (including deanonymizing a
  pseudonymous contributor) without consent
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or PRs
- Anything that would violate GitHub's own Acceptable Use Policies

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **Contact@Cypheranon.com**. Maintainers will review and
respond — ranging from a private word to removal from the project for
repeated or severe violations. We'll handle reports discreetly.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces: issues, PRs, discussions, and anywhere else
someone is representing the project publicly.
